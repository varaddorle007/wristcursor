# Drift Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-11

Drift has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We don't collect personal data.

## What the watch app uses
- **Motion sensor (gyroscope):** moves the pointer. Readings stay on the watch and are never stored.
- **Notifications:** show that Drift is running and give you a Stop button.
- **Battery level and battery saver status:** let Drift pause below 15% battery or under battery saver. Read on the watch only; not stored or sent.
- **Bluetooth:** lets the watch act as a mouse and keyboard for the computer you pair.
- **Wi-Fi:** finds and talks to Drift Receiver on your local network.

## Keyboard and voice
In Keyboard mode, what you type or say is sent only to the computer you're connected to. Speech is turned into text by your watch's own voice-typing service (Google or Samsung), under that service's privacy policy. Drift doesn't keep the text.

## What leaves the watch
Only to the computer you pair:
- **Bluetooth:** pointer moves, clicks, scrolling and left/right arrow keys, like any Bluetooth mouse.
- **Wi-Fi:** the same commands, plus your watch's model name and a random ID Drift creates, so the computer can remember that you allowed this watch.

Nothing is sent to us or to any third party.

## What is stored
- **On the watch:** your settings, the paired computers' names and addresses, and the random ID. They're excluded from cloud backups. Uninstalling Drift deletes them.
- **On the computer:** Drift Receiver remembers the random IDs and model names of the watches you allowed. "Forget watches" deletes them.

## Children
Drift is not directed at children under 13.
