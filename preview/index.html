Connect for real

This website cannot find your computer. Do these steps on the Galaxy Watch + your device.

On the watch

  1. Open WristCursor
  2. Allow Location if asked (needed for Bluetooth scan)
  3. Tap Find computer and leave it open
  4. If the list is empty, tap Scan again

Mac

  1. System Settings → Bluetooth → turn ON
  2. Keep that window open so Mac is discoverable
  3. Look for your watch name and click Connect
  4. On watch: tap the Mac → Connect
  5. Choose Air mouse

Windows PC

  1. Settings → Bluetooth & devices → ON
  2. Add device → Bluetooth
  3. Select your watch
  4. On watch → Connect → Air mouse

Android phone

  1. Phone Bluetooth ON
  2. From watch Find computer, tap the phone → pair
  3. Connect → Air mouse
Tip: Pairing often works better when the computer searches for the watch while Find computer is open on the watch — not only the other way around.
